# Examples

These show the shape, not wording to copy. Every fact in a real post comes from the entry.

## A good post

```
New on the blog: how we cut our build from 12 minutes to 3. Most of it was one cache we had set up wrong.

https://example.com/blog/faster-builds/

#WebPerformance #StaticSites
```

Why it works: one idea in the first line, one more sentence of substance, one link on its own line, two CamelCase hashtags.
About 200 characters, counting the link as 23.

## A post about an event

```
Open studio this Saturday, 10 to 4. Come and see the new press running, ask questions, and take a print home.

Details and directions: https://example.com/events/open-studio/

#OpenStudio #Printmaking
```

The date and times come from the entry. If the entry doesn't say the time, leave it out and say so in the reply.

## What to change

| Draft | Why it falls short | Better |
|---|---|---|
| `Big news!!! We're thrilled to announce our AMAZING new post 🎉🎉🎉` | Hype, no content, emoji string | Say what the post is about in the first sentence |
| `Check it out: https://example.com/a-very-long-address-with-lots-of-words-in-it` with no sentence | No idea to hang the link on | One sentence on what the link holds |
| `#newpost #blog #writing #content #update #news` | Six generic tags, none in CamelCase | `#WebPerformance` |
| `Photo attached.` for an image with no description | Nobody can tell what the image shows | Alt text: "A cast-iron press with a sheet of blue ink drying on the bed." |
| `CW: politics` at the start of the text | It is not a content warning, only text | Tell the person it may need one; they decide |
| Three posts in a thread when one would do | Longer isn't better; people drop out | Cut it to one idea in one post |

## Counting

`Read the full write-up at https://example.com/some/very/long/path/that/goes/on/and/on/forever.html` counts as the words
plus 23 for the address, so a long address costs the same as a short one. Don't shorten a link just to save space.
